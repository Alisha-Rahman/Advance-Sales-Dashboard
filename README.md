# 📊 SuperStore Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard created using SuperStore sales data.

The dashboard helps analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, shipping methods, payment modes, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard that helps analyze sales trends, profit performance, and business patterns.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query

## 💡 Skills Used

- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization
- DAX
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- Time-Series Analysis
- Sales Analysis
- Sales Forecasting

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses need to monitor sales and profitability across different products, customers, regions, and time periods. Raw sales data can make it difficult to identify trends, compare performance, and understand areas that may require attention.

This dashboard addresses the problem by transforming raw SuperStore sales data into an interactive visual report that helps users:

### Page 1 – Sales Analysis

- 💰 **Sales, Orders, Profit & Ship Days KPIs**  
  KPI cards are used to provide a quick overview of the main business metrics.

- 👥 **Sales by Customer Segment**  
  A donut chart is used to compare the contribution of different customer segments to total sales.

- 📈 **Monthly Sales Year-over-Year (YoY)**  
  A line chart is used to compare monthly sales trends across different years and identify changes in sales performance over time.

- 🚚 **Sales by Ship Mode**  
  A bar chart is used to compare sales across different shipping methods.

- 🗺️ **Sales & Profit by State**  
  A map visualization is used to understand the geographical distribution of sales and profit across different states.

- 💳 **Sales by Payment Mode**  
  A donut chart is used to show the proportion of sales generated through different payment methods.

- 📦 **Sales by Category & Sub-Category**  
  A bar chart is used to compare sales across product categories and sub-categories.

- 📅 **Monthly Profit Year-over-Year**  
  A line chart is used to compare monthly profit trends across years and observe changes in profitability over time.

### Page 2 – Sales Forecast

- 📈 **Sales Trend by Order Date**  
  A line chart is used to visualize historical sales movement over time.

- 🔮 **15-Day Sales Forecast**  
  A line chart with forecasting is used to estimate the potential sales trend for the next 15 days based on historical patterns.

- 🗺️ **Sales by State**  
  A map visualization is used to show the geographical distribution of sales.

- 📊 **Historical Sales Trend Analysis**  
  A line chart is used to analyze historical sales patterns and identify changes in sales over time.


## 💡 Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard helps provide business insights by:

- 📈 Identifying changes and trends in monthly sales and profit
- 👥 Understanding sales contribution from different customer segments
- 🗺️ Comparing sales and profit performance across different states
- 📦 Identifying differences in performance across product categories and sub-categories
- 🚚 Understanding sales distribution across different shipping methods
- 💳 Analyzing sales distribution across different payment methods
- 📊 Comparing historical sales patterns across different time periods
- 🔮 Using Power BI forecasting to support future sales trend analysis
